ARROW PUZZLE - PRIVACY POLICY
================================
Effective Date: October 14, 2026
Version: 1.0.0
Contact: vatsalp.dev06@gmail.com

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Arrow Puzzle ("the App", "we", "us") is a 100% offline, single-player logic puzzle
game. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the App
handles, how it is used, and your rights.

**Key promise: The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.
All data stays on your device.**

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DATA WE STORE LOCALLY (ON YOUR DEVICE ONLY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The App uses Android's SharedPreferences (encrypted, app-private storage) to
persist the following between sessions:

A. App Settings (settings.v1)
   - Sound on/off
   - Music on/off (currently unused, reserved)
   - Haptics on/off
   - Color-blind mode on/off
   - Reduce motion on/off
   - Dot grid visibility on/off
   - Theme preference (Light / Dark / System)
   - Language preference (English / Hindi / Gujarati)

B. Game Progress (progress.v1)
   - Current campaign level reached (1-500)
   - Hint tokens remaining (0-99)
   - Stars earned per level (1-3 stars)
   - Lifetime statistics:
       * Total levels cleared
       * Total taps made
       * Wrong taps (blocked arrow taps)
       * Flawless wins (cleared with 3 hearts)
       * No-hint wins (cleared without using a hint)
       * Hard-tier levels cleared
       * Daily challenge levels cleared
       * Best daily streak
   - Set of completed daily challenge dates (YYYY-MM-DD)
   - Set of completed monthly challenge months (YYYY-MM)

C. Saved Game State (savedgame.v1)
   - Current level parameters (seed, size, tier, tag)
   - IDs of arrows already removed from the board
   - Hearts remaining (0-3)
   - Hints used in current session (0+)

D. Onboarding Flag (onboarded.v1)
   - Whether the How-to-Play tutorial has been completed

**None of the above ever leaves your device.** No server, cloud, or third party
receives this data.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PERSONAL INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The App does NOT collect, request, or process any of the following:
- Name, email, phone number, or account credentials
- Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID)
- Location data (GPS, coarse, or network-based)
- Contacts, call logs, SMS, or calendar
- Camera, microphone, or sensor data
- Biometric data
- Payment or financial information

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. NETWORK ACCESS & INTERNET PERMISSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The release build of Arrow Puzzle **does not declare the INTERNET permission**
in its AndroidManifest.xml. The App cannot and does not make any network
requests. All game logic, level generation, and asset loading happen entirely
offline on the device.

The only network-related capability is the optional **in-app update check**
powered by Google Play Core (in_app_update package). When you open the App,
Google Play may silently check if a newer version is available. This uses the
Play Store's existing connection and does not send any personal data to us.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THIRD-PARTY SDKS & LIBRARIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The App bundles only the following runtime dependencies:

- flutter (Google) — UI framework; runs entirely on-device
- shared_preferences (Flutter team) — local key-value storage on device
- audioplayers (community) — plays bundled .wav sound files locally
- in_app_update (Google Play Core) — checks Play Store for app updates

**No analytics SDKs (Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.)**
**No advertising SDKs (AdMob, Unity Ads, ironSource, etc.)**
**No crash reporting SDKs (Crashlytics, Sentry, etc.)**
**No social login or sharing SDKs**

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA / GDPR-K)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The App is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).
Since no personal data is collected at all, COPPA and GDPR-K obligations are
trivially satisfied.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. YOUR RIGHTS & CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Because all data resides solely on your device, you have full control:

- **View / Modify Settings** → Open Settings screen in the App.
- **Reset All Progress** → Settings → "Reset Progress" (clears levels, stats,
  hints, daily history, saved game).
- **Delete All App Data** → Android Settings → Apps → Arrow Puzzle → Storage →
  "Clear Storage" (removes everything including settings).
- **Uninstall** → Removes the App and all its data from your device.

We cannot access, modify, or delete your data on your behalf because we never
receive it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. DATA RETENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Local data persists until you explicitly reset it (via the in-app button) or
uninstall the App. There is no automatic expiration or server-side deletion
because no server exists.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Data is stored in the app's private SharedPreferences directory, inaccessible
  to other apps (Android sandbox).
- No encryption keys are managed by us; Android handles filesystem encryption.
- The App does not write to external (public) storage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If future versions change data practices (e.g., add cloud sync, analytics, or
accounts), this policy will be updated and the version number incremented. You
will be notified in-app before any new data collection begins.
